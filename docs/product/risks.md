# Risks & Legal Caveats

Consolidated from the topic docs so the real-world risks aren't scattered. These are acknowledged risks, not blockers — check the rules for your target jurisdiction before running against real brands, real companies, or real money.

## Revenue-share framing

Economically the 80% is strong. Legally/payment-wise, don't casually market it as "sharing ad revenue" until the rules for target jurisdictions have been checked (securities, payment/KYC, promotion/gambling rules all potentially apply). For the prototype, **"listener rewards funded by verified attention"** is the cleaner product framing.

## Unsolicited AI-generated ads (cold-start engine)

Generating an unsolicited ad for a real company and showing it publicly carries trademark, defamation, and right-of-publicity risk — arguably higher than the revenue-share framing, and it varies by jurisdiction. Before this runs against real companies:

- keep generated content clearly labelled as unofficial/AI-generated and parody where applicable
- honor takedown/opt-out requests immediately
- avoid implying endorsement
- check the rules for target jurisdictions

For the prototype, prefer fictional brands or companies that have opted in. See [content.md](content.md#free-ai-generated-ads-cold-start-engine).

## Payouts

The demo intentionally uses an internal listener balance and defers the actual withdrawal rail to Wave 2, avoiding turning the hackathon into a payments/KYC project. See [economics.md](economics.md#listener-rewards-start-with-an-internal-balance).
