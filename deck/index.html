05 — Privacy mechanics Judgeslide

Dual ledger, doing exactly what it is for

Private — witness

  • Listener secretephemeral, per listener per segment; lives only in private state, rotated every submission
  • Claim secretephemeral, per claim; binds the claim to one consumed attention nullifier
  • Brand’s exact spendhash("slopstream:spend:" || cents) — commitment only, amount never lands

Public — ledger

  • Nullifiersreplay-protecting, unlinkable to a person
  • verifiedCountaggregate only — no viewer rows
  • thresholdMetthe fact the buyer is paying for
  • qualified flagboolean; plus pool totals + claim count

Never on-chain

  • Identityno account, no device graph, no profile store
  • Answersgrading is private to the verifier
  • Weightsper-listener reward share stays off-chain by design
  • Budgetscampaign spend and bids are not disclosed to rivals

Segment / challenge binding is proven in-circuit, never disclosed. Three facts go public; nothing identifies a human.

06 — Falsifiability /verifypublic

Verify it yourself. Run zero of our code.

Every midnight receipt links to /verify?nullifier=…. The page reads public preprod indexer state, prints the raw state SHA-256 before the verdict, and offers a console snippet, a terminal one-liner and an AI prompt — three independent paths to the same answer.

“No record” is only ever shown after a successful read. A failed or unauthenticated read can never render as absence — that is the whole point of the page.

Honest limits — stated on the page

  • Backend ledger is authoritative for clearing during the hackathon; on-chain records are corroborating evidence + non-repudiation, not independent settlement. Yet.
  • BidClearing is a declared sketch, not a hidden one — we say so rather than ship a hollow contract.
  • Not audited. Preprod, not mainnet.
  • Listener rewards are ledger points. No USD value promise, nothing transferable — no money-transmitter framing.
  • Anti-Sybil is real but not perfect: dual 4-deep replay windows, per-session commitment, claim rate limits. Non-trivial, not Sybil-proof.
